# Metal Archives as a source

A single-ticket effort, held until milestone 1 is complete. See `issues/01-metal-archives-as-a-source.md`.

The short version: the two Sources version 1 discovers from are curated, and between them found 28 releases in the week of 8–14 September 2026. Metal Archives lists 280 for the same week, at day granularity, through a JSON endpoint that accepts a past date range. It is the source Ben checks by hand, and the obscure releases it carries are the ones the project exists to stop him missing.

It is held rather than started because three of the loop's four actions are still fake, and because two of its questions — whether a structured Source should bypass model extraction, and whether to read a site whose `robots.txt` disallows the named AI crawlers — are Ben's to answer rather than an agent's to assume.
